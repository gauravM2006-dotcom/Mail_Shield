What is Local Cache Quota?

Mail Shield caches synchronized message snippets, headers, attachment metadata, and threat scan audit logs in the local database. This enables sub-millisecond rule scanning, instant offline keyword searches, and prevents hitting remote provider API rate limits.

Why does it have a quota?

The 5 GB quota ensures that caching stays lean, performant, and does not overwhelm your local system storage.
