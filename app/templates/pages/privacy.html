Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026
1. Our Core Privacy Philosophy

Mail Shield was engineered from the ground up on principles of data minimization and complete algorithmic transparency. We never train machine learning models on your email, never sell your information, and never share data with third parties.

2. Read-Only Mailbox Access

When connecting Gmail, Outlook, Yahoo, or iCloud mailboxes, Mail Shield requests strictly read-only access (`gmail.readonly`, `Mail.Read`, IMAP read). Mail Shield does not possess the technical ability or authorization to send, delete, move, or modify emails on your remote mail servers.

3. Data Retention and Deletion

Cached messages and scan histories are retained according to your customizable retention policy (default 30–90 days). Disconnecting a mailbox immediately purges all associated messages from our database. Deleting your account permanently deletes all user records, credentials, and settings.

4. Google API User Data Policy Compliance

Mail Shield's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
