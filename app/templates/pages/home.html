Customer Trust & Architecture

How Mail Shield Works

Designed from the ground up to respect your confidentiality. We believe email security shouldn't require surrendering your privacy.

1. Strict Read-Only

Mail Shield only connects via read-only OAuth. We never request permission to send, edit, forward, or delete any of your emails.

2. In-Memory Analysis

Email bodies and headers are parsed in ephemeral memory buffers during detection. Your email content is never permanently stored on our disks.

3. Zero AI Training

We never send your correspondence to public LLMs or use your communications to train AI models. Your inbox remains strictly your private domain.

4. One-Click Revocation

You maintain 100% control. Disconnect your mailbox at any time from your settings to instantly wipe all tokens and cached scan metadata.
